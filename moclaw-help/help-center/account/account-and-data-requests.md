# Account And Data Requests

Use **Settings > Account** to see your signed-in identity and current plan.

The current Account section does not edit your email, display name, or avatar,
and current help sources do not show self-serve controls for deleting an
account, exporting data, or deleting account data.

## Change Profile Information

If you need to change your email, display name, or avatar, first check whether
your sign-in provider controls that information. If the change is not available
there or in MoClaw, contact support.

MoClaw may display identity from your verified sign-in email or sign-in provider
profile. Changing the provider profile may be the right first step when the
email, display name, or avatar comes from that provider.

## Delete Account Or Data

For account deletion, data deletion, data export, retention, DPA, GDPR, CCPA,
model-training-policy questions, or other legal/privacy requests, contact
support so the request can be routed to the right owner-reviewed process.

Start from the account you want support to review when possible. If you cannot
sign in, include the account email and describe the sign-in issue, but do not
send passwords, verification codes, tokens, cookies, or browser storage.

Include:

- account email used to sign in;
- request type;
- short description of what you want changed, deleted, exported, or reviewed;
- whether you can still sign in;
- whether you have an active Pro subscription, open billing issue, or recent
  checkout;
- preferred contact email if different from your account email.

Do not send passwords, full card numbers, CVV, API keys, OAuth tokens, device
codes, government IDs, browser storage, full raw logs, or unrelated personal
data in the first support response.

## Billing Is Separate

Deleting account data, canceling Pro, and requesting a refund are separate
operations. If you have an active paid subscription, check **Settings > Account**
for subscription actions and **Settings > Billing** for payment history.

Do not assume an account/data request automatically cancels a subscription,
removes invoices or payment history, or creates a refund. Mention any active
subscription, recent checkout, invoice, or refund concern so support can route
the billing part separately.
