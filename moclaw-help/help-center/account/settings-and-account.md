# Settings And Account

Open the user menu in the lower-left corner of the left sidebar, then choose
**Settings**.

On desktop, Settings uses tabs. On mobile, Settings appears as one scrollable
sheet with the same sections stacked vertically.

## General

Use **Settings > General** to change:

- language;
- send shortcut;
- timezone;
- 12-hour or 24-hour time format;
- browser notifications.

Desktop notifications require your browser or operating system to allow
notifications. If the browser permission is denied, the notification setting may
not stay on.

Language and other display preferences are stored in the browser. If you clear
browser storage, use private/incognito mode, switch browsers, or move to another
device, these settings may reset. On first load, MoClaw uses a supported saved
language if available, then your browser language when supported, then English.

Timezone and time format affect visible chat timestamps. If a timestamp looks
wrong, check **Settings > General > Timezone** first and verify the selected
IANA timezone.

## Appearance

Use **Settings > Appearance** to change theme, font, text size, spacing, and
color style. Use **Reset** in this section to restore appearance defaults.

Appearance **Reset** only resets theme, font, text size, spacing, and color. It
does not reset language, send shortcut, timezone, notifications, usage display,
account state, billing state, or API keys.

## Account

Use **Settings > Account** to see your signed-in identity and current plan. This
section can also show plan actions such as upgrading, canceling Pro, or keeping
a subscription that is ending soon. Current public support posture is no public
30-day no-card MoClaw Trial.

The current Account section does not edit your email, display name, or avatar.
If you need an identity change that is not available in the app, contact support
or update it through your sign-in provider when applicable.

For account deletion, data deletion, data export, retention, or legal/privacy
requests, contact support so the request can be routed to the right
owner-reviewed process.

Account deletion, subscription cancellation, and refunds are separate requests.
Use the visible subscription controls for Pro cancellation/reactivation, and
include any billing concern when contacting support about account/data requests.

## Usage Badge

Use **Settings > Usage** to control whether the credits badge is shown on the
New Chat header. Session and feature pages do not show this badge. The same
section also shows your available credits and credit history. This display
preference is local to your browser.

## Privacy, Terms, And Logout

Privacy Policy, Terms, and Logout are available from the lower-left user menu.
The **Settings > About** section also links to Privacy Policy and Terms.
