# Chat Channels

Channels are places where you can talk to MoClaw outside the main web chat when
they are available for your account and environment.

Examples that appear in current product sources include Telegram, Slack, Lark,
and Discord. Availability can vary, so use the current **left sidebar > Channels**
section as the source for your account.

## Channels vs Connectors

Channels and connectors are different:

- A **Channel** is where you chat with MoClaw.
- A **Connector** gives MoClaw access to a third-party service or data source.

For example, chatting with MoClaw in Slack is not the same as giving MoClaw
access to every Slack workspace or message.

## Where To Check

1. Open `/chat`.
2. Open the left sidebar.
3. Expand **Channels**.
4. Look for the channel you want to use.
5. Follow the connect or bind flow if the row is available.

For channel-specific binding steps, see
`channels/connect-chat-channels.md`.

## If A Channel Is Missing

The channel may not be enabled for your account, environment, region, or product
plan. Ask support with:

- Account email.
- The channel you expected.
- Screenshot of **left sidebar > Channels**.
- The task you want to do through that channel.

Do not assume a channel is generally launched for everyone just because it
appears in a screenshot, code path, or product discussion.

Agent-owned email or SMS, when discussed, is separate from chat channels. Use
the agent email/phone article for that availability boundary.
