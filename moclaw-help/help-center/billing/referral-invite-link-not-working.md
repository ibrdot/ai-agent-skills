# Referral Invite Link Not Working

Referral links are feature-gated and reviewed before credits are granted.
Opening a link or signing up does not instantly grant referral credits.

## If The Invite Is Invalid

Ask the inviter to copy a fresh referral link from MoClaw and send you that
link again. The link may be missing, expired, inactive, or no longer valid.

## If Referrals Are Not Available

If the page says **Referrals are not available yet**, referrals may not be
enabled for your account or environment. Continue from the normal sign-in page
or contact support with the page you are viewing.

## If The Browser Cannot Save The Invite

If you see **We could not save this invite in your browser**, keep the invite
page open, enable browser storage, or retry in a normal non-private browser
window. MoClaw needs temporary browser storage to carry the invite through login.

## If You Signed In But The Invite Was Not Claimed

Open the original invite link again and try once more. If it still fails, the
invite may be ineligible or waiting for review.

Referral rewards require eligibility and review. They are not instant, and they
do not extend Pro or Trial entitlement.

If the invitee has already used MoClaw before accepting the invite, has already
received a referral reward, is outside the bind window, is using an unsupported
account type, or is trying to accept their own invite, the link can be
ineligible even if it opened successfully.

## What To Send Support

- Whether you are the inviter or invitee.
- Account email, if known.
- The visible invite page state or error.
- Approximate time and timezone.
- Whether you were using a private/incognito window or browser storage was
  blocked.

Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, cookies, full browser storage, or unrelated
personal data.
