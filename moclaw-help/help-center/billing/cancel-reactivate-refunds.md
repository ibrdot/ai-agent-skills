# Cancel, Reactivate, Or Ask About A Refund

Open **Settings > Account** to manage your current plan action.

If you have active paid Pro, you may see **Cancel**. If your paid Pro plan is
already set to cancel at the end of the billing period, you may see
**Resubscribe** or **Keep subscription**. If the account is not paid Pro, the
same area may show the paywall or **Upgrade to Pro** instead.

## Cancel Pro

1. Open `/chat`.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Go to **Account**.
4. Find the current plan row.
5. Click **Cancel** if the action is available.
6. Confirm the cancellation dialog.
7. Refresh Settings after the action completes.

Paid Pro cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period.
You can keep using paid Pro until that period ends, and remaining credits are
preserved according to the current credit rules.

Trialing Pro cancellation is immediate and revokes remaining trial credits. Do
not treat that as proof of a current public 30-day no-card MoClaw Trial.

## Reactivate Pro

If your paid Pro subscription is already canceling but still active, use
**Resubscribe** or **Keep subscription** from **Settings > Account**.
You may also see **Keep subscription** from the New Chat Usage badge or
**Settings > Usage** when the account is in a canceling state.

If the subscription has fully ended, reactivation may not be available from the
old subscription state. Subscribe again from checkout.

If **Keep subscription** or **Resubscribe** fails, refresh **Settings >
Account**. The old subscription may already have ended, or billing state may
need to sync. If it still fails, contact support with the visible error and
plan state.

## Refund Questions

Cancellation and refund are different operations. The current help knowledge
base does not contain an owner-reviewed public refund policy, and the reviewed
product sources do not show a self-serve refund/proration button. Refund
outcome, proration, charge disputes, and invoice corrections need billing
review.

The **Cancel anytime** pricing copy means the product allows cancellation. It
does not by itself promise an automatic refund, proration, chargeback result, or
invoice correction.

When contacting support, include:

- Account email.
- Charge amount and charge date.
- Invoice, receipt, or checkout reference if visible.
- Current plan state shown in **Settings > Account**.
- Whether you canceled, reactivated, upgraded, or bought credits.
- Exact visible error copy if the cancel or keep-subscription action failed.

Do not send full card numbers, CVV, passwords, API keys, or OAuth tokens.

The current UI does not show a customer-facing Stripe Billing Portal button.
