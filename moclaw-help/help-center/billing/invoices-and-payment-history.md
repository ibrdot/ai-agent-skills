# Invoices And Payment History

Open **Settings > Billing** to view your payment history.

## What You Can See

The payment-history section can show:

- subscription payments and renewals;
- Credit Pack purchases;
- payment date, amount, and status;
- a PDF download when one is available;
- a hosted invoice or receipt link when one is available.

Use the tabs to switch between **All**, **Subscription**, and **Credit Pack**
entries.

## If A Link Is Missing

Some payment rows may not have both a PDF and hosted invoice link. That usually
means the billing provider did not return that link for the row. It does not
mean the payment failed.

## If You Do Not See A Payment

Try these checks:

1. Make sure you are on **Settings > Billing**.
2. Switch the tab back to **All**.
3. Use **Retry** if the payment history failed to load.
4. Confirm you are signed in with the account that made the payment.

For refund, duplicate charge, invoice correction, or charge-dispute questions,
contact billing support with your account email, charge date, charge amount,
and any visible invoice or receipt reference.
