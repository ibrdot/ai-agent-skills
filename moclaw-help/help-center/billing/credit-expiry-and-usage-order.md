# Credit Expiry And Usage Order

Open **Settings > Usage** to see your available credits, credit buckets, and
recent credit history.

## Usage Order

MoClaw uses credits in this order:

1. bonus credits;
2. subscription or trial credits;
3. purchased Credit Pack credits.

When there are multiple credit sources of the same type, MoClaw uses the
earliest-expiring source first.

So if you have both current subscription credits and purchased Credit Pack
credits, MoClaw normally uses the subscription credits before the Credit Pack.

## Expiry

- Bonus credits may expire. Referral bonus credits currently expire after 3
  days once granted.
- Subscription or trial credits expire at the end of the current billing or
  trial period.
- Purchased Credit Pack credits currently use a 1-year expiry in the billing
  implementation.

The most reliable place to check your own account is **Settings > Usage**,
especially the credit bucket and credit-history dates.

If a Credit Pack row does not show the date you expected, collect the purchase
amount, approximate purchase date, and what **Settings > Usage** shows before
assuming the credits never expire.

## If Credits Are Visible But Not Usable

This page only explains expiry and consumption order. If your credits are
visible but chat is blocked, check `billing/pricing-credits-trials.md` and
`troubleshooting/common-errors.md` because product access and credit balance are
handled separately.
